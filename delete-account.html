Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 2026

1. About this page

This page explains how to delete your Stackwax account and the personal data associated with it. Stackwax is developed by Martin Allais, based in Barcelona, Spain. You can contact us at support@stackwax.app.

2. Delete in the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from Stackwax:

  • Open the Stackwax app on iOS or Android
  • Tap the settings icon in the top right of the main screen
  • Scroll to Account and tap Delete Account
  • Confirm the action. Your account and data are scheduled for permanent deletion

3. Request by email

If you cannot access the app, you may request account deletion by email:

  • Send an email to support@stackwax.app
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Include the email address associated with your Stackwax account so we can locate it
  • We will confirm the deletion within 7 business days

4. What gets deleted

When you delete your Stackwax account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your user account, including email address and hashed password
  • Your entire music library, including all shelved tracks, BPM, key, and metadata
  • Your recognition history and records of past track lookups
  • Any personal identifiers linked to your account

5. What may be retained

Some data may be retained for a limited period after account deletion:

  • Anonymized aggregated usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you
  • Legal, tax, or fraud-prevention records where retention is required by law
  • Third party services such as recognition providers may retain anonymized query logs per their own retention policies. These are not linked to your Stackwax identity

6. Retention period

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. After processing, your data is permanently removed from all active systems. Backup copies are purged within a further 30 days.
